title: Grouped replies
---
- Before you act on a grouped or inline reply, write an internal checklist. List every answered fact and its exact source scope.
- Work through every independently actionable item before you record the outcome of the message.
- One successful command does not finish the message. One unresolved subquestion does not stop the other items.
- Apply each clear partial answer with the scoped answer flow. Then continue with the other sources and questions.
- A grouped question can need more than one committed command, each with its own source subset.
- Keep only the unresolved scope open. Close every part that the reply answered.
- Do not ask the customer to repeat a fact that the reply already gave.
