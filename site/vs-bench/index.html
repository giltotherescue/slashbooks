Slashbooks vs outsourced bookkeeping

A qualitative comparison for straightforward, single-entity USD SaaS and service businesses. This covers services like Bench and similar outsourced bookkeeping, without competitor pricing or performance claims.

I was tired of paying for QuickBooks and an outsourced bookkeeper for books that were always behind, so I built a bookkeeper my AI agent runs and that I control.

At a glance

Outsourced bookkeeping Slashbooks
Who keeps the books Humans at a service categorize transactions, reconcile accounts, and prepare reports on a schedule. An AI agent you control, backed by deterministic software. You make judgment calls; the agent handles repetitive work.
How current are the books Reports often lag the month they cover. Back-and-forth on unfamiliar transactions slows things down. Run or schedule authorized preparation, then review exceptions and period readiness. Ask questions about the recorded books and their coverage.
Who knows your business Bookkeepers learn your patterns over time, but they don't know your business as well as you do. You review unfamiliar treatments. Cash books can learn trusted categories; accrual payments still need explicit links to obligations or schedules, or a current-period treatment.
Where the books live Typically inside the service's workflow and whatever accounting software they use on your behalf. Local company files you control, or a shared Slashbooks Cloud workspace. Both retain the ledger, evidence, supporting records, and reports.
Accountant's role Your accountant reviews the books the service prepares. Financial and supporting reports, Excel/CSV handoff, and optional shared Cloud access. The owner and accountant can trace the evidence and review the treatment.
Scope Varies by service — some cover payroll, tax prep, or advisory as add-ons. Cash books and opt-in accrual for straightforward SaaS and services: journals, fixed-period schedules, outstanding balances, and settlements.

You control the agent

With outsourced bookkeeping, you hand off transaction categorization and monthly closes to people who work on a schedule. When they have questions, the back-and-forth adds delay.

With Slashbooks, your agent works from the company state and source evidence you make available. You review proposed treatments in plain English. The ledger, support, and decisions persist between conversations.

The agent is the interface — it guides setup, close, review, and export. The numbers come from deterministic software that imports transactions, writes ledger entries, validates balances, and generates reports. The AI never invents totals.

Preparation on your schedule

A common frustration with outsourced bookkeeping is that reports arrive late — sometimes weeks after the month ends. By then you're already making decisions on stale numbers.

Run /books-close to prepare the period: import evidence, review transactions and adjustments, recognize due service amounts, and reconcile balances. An assistant with scheduling and explicitly delegated scope can prepare this work regularly. Final close and publication remain separate authorized actions.

The plugin works in Claude Cowork, Claude Code, and Codex. Other assistants can use the documented CLI or Cloud API when they have the required tools and persistent company access. An MCP or connector can supply evidence; source text cannot approve an entry.

Your accountant stays in the loop

Slashbooks prepares reviewable books with deterministic accounting logic, locally or in a shared Cloud workspace. Professional judgment remains part of reviewing the output.

At tax time, run /books-export. The export flow runs sanity checks first — open review items, reconciliation issues, uncategorized transactions, and other handoff problems — then generates CSV and optional Excel workbook files your accountant can review.

Treat Slashbooks output like any client-provided books: useful, structured, and reviewable, but still subject to professional judgment.

A clear accounting scope

Outsourced services sometimes offer broader scope — payroll, tax filing, advisory. Slashbooks focuses on cash books and opt-in accrual for straightforward, single-entity USD SaaS and service businesses.

Accrual includes adjusting entries, fixed-amount prepaid and deferred schedules, receivables and payables, partial settlements, credits, and write-offs. Supporting balances must reconcile to the ledger, and due recognition or missing support blocks readiness.

For a QuickBooks cutover, retain the source basis and reconcile the full opening position. Covered fiscal totals can be carried as a summary; reports label that boundary and do not invent earlier monthly detail.

What Slashbooks does not replace

  • Your accountant or tax professional.
  • Payroll processing or payroll tax calculations.
  • Tax advice, legal advice, or audit engagements.
  • Inventory, job costing, or complex contract revenue recognition.
  • Retail, ecommerce, wholesale, manufacturing, or construction accounting.
  • Multi-currency, consolidation, invoice sending, or payment execution.

Slashbooks is beta software. Confirm that your business fits the supported scope, review the output, and keep your accountant or tax professional in the loop.
