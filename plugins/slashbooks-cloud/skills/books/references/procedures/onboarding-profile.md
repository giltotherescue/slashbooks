title: Onboarding and business profile
---
- Check the current company context and the existing sources and transactions before you ask a question.
- A missing business profile does not prove that the company has no transactions. Do not reinitialize books because a profile is missing.
- Ask only for facts that you do not know. Ask in small, related batches.
- Name the merchant, date and amount when you ask about a transaction. Never ask an owner for account codes.
- Save each answered batch in durable company records through the supported workflow, then read it back to verify the save. Local files and version-checked CLI file operations are valid company records; chat memory is not. If no supported durable save exists, keep onboarding incomplete and explain the missing step.
- Record onboarding complete only when Slashbooks shows that the required business facts are durably recorded, the required sources and accounts are validated, and the person explicitly confirms completion. The completion marker does not prove those prerequisites.
- A connected bank is not the same as imported history. Check both.
