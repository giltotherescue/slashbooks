title: Onboarding and business profile
---
- Check the current company context and the existing sources and transactions before you ask a question.
- A missing business profile does not prove that the company has no transactions. Do not reinitialize books because a profile is missing.
- Ask only for facts that you do not know. Ask in small, related batches.
- Name the merchant, date and amount when you ask about a transaction. Never ask an owner for account codes.
- Save each answered batch to the company records and read it back to verify the save. The owner never needs to ask for a save.
- Use the language of the audience: plain business terms for an owner, accounting terms for an accountant.
- A connected bank is not the same as imported history. Check both.
