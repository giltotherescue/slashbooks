# Prepare 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC

Slashbooks prepares a payment review, a CPA handoff package and printable recipient
copies. It does not file federal or state returns, distribute copies, determine
worker status or choose tax classifications. The customer or preparer must review
confirmed facts, file the returns and distribute approved recipient copies.

## Supported rules

The dependency-free `bookkeeping.tax1099.prepare_1099(payload)` function supports
explicit tax years 2025 (`2025.1`) and 2026 (`2026.1`). Other years and mismatched
rule versions fail. It accepts single-payer US business payments in USD.

| Confirmed payment category | Form and box | 2025 minimum | 2026 minimum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nonemployee compensation or legal services | NEC 1 (2025), 1a (2026) | $600 | $2,000 |
| Rent | MISC 1 | $600 | $2,000 |
| Royalties | MISC 2 | $10 | $10 |
| Other income | MISC 3 | $600 | $2,000 |
| Medical and health care | MISC 6 | $600 | $2,000 |
| Attorney gross proceeds | MISC 10 | $600 | $600 |

Federal withholding causes the associated form to be prepared below its ordinary
threshold. Mixed NEC/MISC withholding requires a resolved allocation. The ordinary
corporation exemption applies except to supported legal, medical and attorney
proceeds categories. LLCs must have a verified tax classification; an LLC label
alone is insufficient. Foreign recipients, exempt classifications and unsupported
categories remain visible as issues for professional handling. No exemption,
employee status, payer responsibility or tax election is inferred from a name or
an account. State withholding is unsupported and blocks readiness.

Rules are grounded in the [2025 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/i1099mec--2025.pdf)
and [2026 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099mec). These bounded
categories do not represent all boxes or exceptions in those instructions.

## Paid-fact contract

The top-level object contains `tax_year`, optional `rule_version`, `payer_profile`,
`recipients`, `payments` and `coverage`.

- `payer_profile`: stable `id`, `legal_name`, `address`, `phone`, `verified: true`,
  `tin_status: "verified"`. `payer_type` defaults to `ordinary_business`;
  other payer types require professional handling.
- Each recipient: stable `party_id`, `legal_name`, `address`,
  `tax_classification`, `classification_verified: true`,
  `w9_status: "verified"`, `tin_status: "verified"`; optional W-9 evidence ID.
  Supported classifications are `individual`, `sole_proprietor`, `partnership`,
  `estate`, `c_corporation` and `s_corporation`.
- Address: `line1`, optional `line2`, `city`, `region`, `postal_code`,
  `country: "US"`.
- Each payment: `id`, `party_id`, ISO `date`, `currency: "USD"`, `gross_amount`,
  `cash_amount`, `federal_withholding`, `direction: "pay"`, `payment_method`,
  `source: {source_id, bank_account, mode}`, nonempty `evidence_ids`, and
  `allocations`. Amounts are exact decimal strings, never floats.
- Each allocation: `amount`, `tax_category`, `classification_verified: true`,
  optional `item_id` and evidence IDs. Categories are the snake-case names
  `nonemployee_compensation`, `legal_services`, `rent`, `royalties`,
  `other_income`, `medical_healthcare`, `attorney_gross_proceeds` and
  `not_reportable`. Unknown or unverified classifications block that allocation.
- `coverage`: `complete: true`, `opening_payments_complete: true`,
  `start_date: "YYYY-01-01"`, `end_date: "YYYY-12-31"`. A partial-year import
  cannot establish complete calendar-year coverage. Opening year-to-date paid
  facts need their own source and evidence IDs.

Gross equals cash plus withholding and equals the sum of allocations. Receipts,
bank imports, direct vendor payments and bill settlements use the same canonical
paid-fact contract. Unpaid bills do not enter this input. No expense-ledger total
is substituted for payment evidence.

Methods are `bank_transfer`, `check`, `cash`, `card`, `payment_network` and
`unknown`. Confirmed card payments are excluded for payment-settlement-entity
reporting. Network payments require explicit boolean `network_qualifies_1099k`;
only `true` causes that exclusion. An unknown rail or network treatment blocks
readiness. Processor payments with payer withholding require review.

Exact canonical or source duplicates are counted once. Conflicting identity
copies are all blocked. Bank/settlement/import adapters must preserve the same
canonical ID or source identity for one payment; unrelated aliases cannot be
resolved by matching names or amounts.

Refunds are positive amounts with `direction: "receive"` and `refund_of` naming
the original canonical payment. Same-year refunds reduce the original categories
only when recipient, method, classification, dates and cumulative category amounts
agree. Prior-year refunds, unsupported withholding adjustments and excessive
refunds block readiness; they never silently change an earlier tax year.

## Review and snapshots

The result includes `input_hash`, `content_hash`, explicit year/rule version,
safe payer/recipient profiles, `forms` with Decimal-derived box amounts,
`payments` with included/excluded/blocked allocation detail and provenance,
`issues`, `coverage` and reconciled `totals`. Mixed payments retain separate
allocation statuses. `ready` is false when any unresolved issue remains.
Recipient identity, W-9 and TIN completeness block actual reportable forms;
an entirely excluded or below-threshold payee does not require a TIN merely to
finish this preparation. Unknown category decisions still block their amounts.

The result starts as `status: "prepared"`. The caller stores an immutable
snapshot and owns authenticated review authorization. A reviewed snapshot sets
`status: "reviewed"` without changing the report facts. Both export helpers verify
`content_hash`; changing totals, profiles or evidence invalidates the snapshot.
The status label is not itself an authorization credential. Record the reviewing
person, snapshot hash and revision history in the caller's protected audit store.
CPA JSON and PDF exports bind the same content hash. Revisions preserve old
snapshots; a new prepared version does not imply a filed correction.

## Tax identifier boundary

Routine inputs and reports contain TIN status only. Raw TIN/SSN/EIN fields,
common key aliases and identifier-shaped values are refused before hashing or
serializing. The bookkeeping workspace and ordinary agent responses must never
receive full identifiers. Source tax documents need a separate protected store;
a general document attachment is not a secure W-9 store.
Typed opaque source, payment, party and evidence IDs may contain unformatted
numeric references. They must be validated as identities and linked to canonical
records, not used as arbitrary free text. Formatted SSNs/EINs remain rejected in
these fields. Names, addresses and other text do not receive this exception.
Unexpected input fields fail explicitly, including unsupported tax amounts;
canonical fee, recognition and excess metadata have a bounded accepted schema.

`export_cpa_package(preparation, output_dir, protected_profiles=None)` creates a
new owner-only directory containing preparation JSON, safe form-totals CSV,
manifest and distribution guidance. Spreadsheet text cells are escaped. Files
are staged privately and published as a complete package under an export lock;
a failed write removes the staging directory. Existing output directories are
never reused. A crash can leave a private staging directory or lock for an
operator to inspect; these are never automatically treated as completed exports.

An explicit privileged call can supply transient
`{payer: {id, tin}, recipients: {party_id: {party_id, tin}}}` to include a separate
protected profile JSON file in that package. All identities must match. This
export must be outside the source repository. The caller must authorize the
request, keep transport and destination private and prevent raw profile values
from entering logs, prompts, command arguments or ordinary workspace state.

## Official recipient PDFs

Install the optional `pdf` extra. Core preparation does not require it.

`render_recipient_pdf(preparation, recipient_id, protected_profile, output)`
accepts transient `{payer: {id, tin}, recipient: {party_id, tin}}` and an explicit
new output path outside the source repository. It renders only that recipient's
forms. Unreviewed output is visibly **DRAFT - NOT FOR DISTRIBUTION**. Reviewed
output requires a ready snapshot. Oversized text and unsupported characters fail
instead of being clipped or silently altered. Latin-script Windows-1252 text is
supported; other scripts require a separately validated rendering path.

Four unmodified official IRS PDFs are shipped in `tax1099_templates/` with source
URLs, revisions and SHA-256 checksums in `manifest.json`. Each tax year has its own
field map. Runtime checks template bytes before use. A fresh writer copies only
Copy B, the original recipient instructions and Copy 2; it never includes Copy A,
other recipient data, attachments or an AcroForm tree. Values are static page text
and files use mode 0600. Publication is atomic and refuses an existing file.

The official [2025 NEC](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/f1099nec--2025.pdf),
[2025 MISC](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/f1099msc--2025.pdf),
[2026 NEC](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f1099nec.pdf) and
[2026 MISC](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f1099msc.pdf) permit printing recipient
copies. See [Publication 1179](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p1179.pdf) for
recipient-statement specifications. The package provides Copy 2 for recipient
use but does not calculate state reporting duties, state income or state
withholding. State reporting remains explicitly `not_prepared`.
Electronic furnishing has recipient-consent requirements. Downloading a PDF does
not establish consent, distribution or filing.

The PDF extra pins pypdf 6.21.4, checked against current package metadata on
October 30, 2026. Earlier 6.21.1 is affected by the upstream
[repeated missing-reference advisory](https://github.com/py-pdf/pypdf/security/advisories/GHSA-6c7f-9265-g8fw),
[cross-reference advisory](https://github.com/py-pdf/pypdf/security/advisories/GHSA-jr96-gvgh-j3r4)
and [large xref limits advisory](https://github.com/py-pdf/pypdf/security/advisories/GHSA-gmh7-3g9r-mjh5).
The renderer accepts only shipped, checksum-verified templates; it is not an
untrusted-PDF intake parser.

Run focused tests with an interpreter containing the optional extra:

```sh
PYTHONPATH=src:tests python3 -m unittest test_tax1099 test_tax1099_pdf
```

The PDF suite checks all four templates, original instructions, field maps,
static output, version hashes, draft/reviewed handling and recipient isolation.
It skips the PDF lane explicitly when the optional dependency is unavailable.
Visual review of generated forms remains necessary after template or renderer
changes; text extraction alone cannot establish layout quality.
